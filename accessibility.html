Inclusive access

Accessibility statement

This personal academic website is intended to be usable by as many people as possible, regardless of technology, ability, or mode of interaction.

Scope and standards

The site is developed with WCAG 2.2 Levels A and AA and the relevant requirements of EN 301 549 as technical targets.

Accessibility measures

  • semantic landmarks and a clear heading hierarchy
  • keyboard operation for navigation and accessibility options
  • visible focus indicators and accessible names for interactive controls
  • responsive reflow on small screens and when content is magnified
  • text alternatives for meaningful images
  • optional controls for text size and spacing, line height, contrast, link highlighting, saturation, images, motion, readable typography, and a large cursor

Privacy

Accessibility preferences are stored only in your browser using local storage and are not transmitted to this site. This site does not deploy its own analytics or tracking scripts. Embedded third-party content, including the map, is subject to the privacy practices of its provider.

Known limitations

Some linked third-party pages and older publication PDF files may not fully satisfy every accessibility requirement. Third-party content is outside the control of this website.

Feedback

If you encounter an accessibility barrier or need information in a different format, please contact gpapad [at] upatras [dot] gr and include the page, the problem, and the assistive technology or device used when relevant.
